# Privacy Policy for Kolay Ticaret Defteri

**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

## 1. Overview
Kolay Ticaret Defteri ("the App") is designed to prioritize user privacy. The App operates as a local-first ledger and offline financial management application.

## 2. Data Collection and Storage
- **Local Storage:** All financial records, customer details, and transaction histories are stored locally on your device using Hive local storage.
- **No Cloud Synchronization:** The App does not automatically upload, transmit, or back up your personal or business data to external servers.
- **No Personal Data Selling:** We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with third parties.

## 3. Permissions
The App may request minimal system permissions strictly required for core functionality (such as local file storage access for export/import functions).

## 4. Third-Party Services
If third-party services (e.g., Google Play Services) are utilized for app distribution or crash analytics, their respective privacy policies apply.
